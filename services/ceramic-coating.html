What Ceramic Coating Does for Your Vehicle

Unlike wax or sealants that sit on the surface, ceramic coating chemically bonds to your paint for semi-permanent protection.

💧

Hydrophobic Surface

Water beads off instantly and takes contaminants with it. Your vehicle stays cleaner longer and washes easier after coating.

☀️

UV Fade Prevention

UV radiation degrades paint and fades color over time. Ceramic coating blocks UV rays, keeping your paint vibrant for years.

🧪

Chemical Resistance

Bird droppings, tree sap, road salt, and industrial fallout can etch unprotected paint. Ceramic coating neutralizes chemical attack.

💎

Gloss Enhancement

The molecular-level bond creates a deep, glass-like gloss that makes paint look better than the day it left the factory.

🛡

Scratch Resistance

9H hardness rating provides genuine scratch resistance against light swirls and wash marring — better than any wax or sealant.

⚡

Graphene Enhancement

Add graphene ceramic technology for superior water spot resistance, lower surface temperature, and extended durability beyond standard ceramic.

Graphene ceramic adds graphene oxide particles to the ceramic formula.
