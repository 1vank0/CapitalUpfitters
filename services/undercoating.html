The Maryland Problem

Road Salt Is Destroying Your Vehicle

Maryland's State Highway Administration applies roughly 200,000+ tons of road salt per winter season. That salt spray coats your vehicle's undercarriage and begins attacking bare metal within weeks. Most automaker factory undercoating is minimal — it doesn't last beyond the warranty period.

Rust doesn't just look bad. Once it reaches structural components like frame rails, control arm mounting points, and sub-frames, you're looking at serious safety issues and expensive repairs. The cost of undercoating is a fraction of what frame rust remediation costs.

  • Frame rail corrosion leads to structural failure
  • Floor pan rust creates interior moisture and safety hazards
  • Wheel well corrosion damages brake lines and fuel lines
  • Rust spreads — early treatment is exponentially cheaper
