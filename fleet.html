Fleet Program Benefits

Built for How Your Business Operates

We've structured our fleet program around the real constraints of managing commercial vehicles — schedule, budget, and consistency.

💵

Volume Pricing

Per-unit cost decreases as your fleet grows. 3–9 vehicles, 10–24 vehicles, and 25+ programs each carry progressively better per-unit pricing. No minimums on individual services.

📅

Priority Scheduling

Fleet vehicles are scheduled ahead of retail walk-in traffic. We work around your operational needs — staggered drop-offs, weekend intake, or multi-vehicle batch scheduling.

🧾

Net-30 Billing

Qualified fleet accounts receive consolidated invoice billing with Net-30 payment terms. No per-vehicle credit card processing. One invoice per billing period covering all units.

🤝

Dedicated Account Manager

One person owns your account. They know your specs, your schedule, and your approval process. No re-explaining your requirements to a different person every time.

📋

Standardized Specs

We document your approved upfit specifications on file. Every vehicle in your fleet gets the identical treatment — consistent branding, consistent protection, consistent quality.

📊

Fleet Reporting

Monthly or quarterly fleet summaries available on request. Track which vehicles were serviced, what was applied, and total spend by vehicle or period.
